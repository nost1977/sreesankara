A Respected Institution in Wayanad

Established in the year 2000, Sree Sankara Vidyamandiram stands as a beacon of culturally rich, harmonious, and value-centered education in Kalpetta. Guided by the noble philosophy of Bharatheeya Vidyanikethan, our institution serves families across nearby panchayats with a mission to build physically, mentally, and spiritually developed learners.

Our vision transcends textbook learning. We prepare students for life and service by instilling our core pillars:

  • Knowledge
  • Values
  • Discipline
  • Awareness
  • Spiritual Guidance
  • Personal Care

Academic Excellence

Nurturing Minds from Pre-KG to Class 7

We provide a strong, NCERT-based curriculum designed to cultivate intellectual curiosity and a deep love for learning. Our supportive and student-friendly classroom approach ensures that education is imparted with both rigor and compassion.

Recognizing the unique potential of each child, our dedicated educators provide personalized attention to learners. We are continuously expanding our academic horizons, building an aspirational framework for the future growth of our student body.

Holistic Education

Developing the Child Intellectually, Physically, Socially, Morally, and Spiritually

The 5 Pillars of Panchanga Shikshan

At Vidyanikethan schools, Panchanga Shikshan is integrated with regular academics to instill a profound sense of values. The guiding principle is that textbook education alone is insufficient for holistic development. The primary objective is to nurture children based on Indian culture and values. The five core components are:

Physical Education (ശാരീരികം)

Exercises and sports training aimed at increasing children's physical fitness and vitality.

Yoga (യോഗ)

Yogasanas and pranayama practice designed to enhance mental concentration and health.

Music (സംഗീതം)

Training in light music, patriotic songs, and classical music to foster creativity in children.

Sanskrit (സംസ്കൃതം)

Learning Sanskrit to deeply understand Indian heritage and gain linguistic proficiency.

Moral Education (നൈതികം)

Value-based education to cultivate ethical values, patriotism, and good virtues in children.

Physical Education & Yoga

Fostering physical vitality and mental tranquility through structured daily practices.

Sanskrit & Culture

Connecting students to their heritage through language and cultural personality development.

Vedic Mathematics

Enhancing numerical agility and cognitive sharpness with traditional mathematical techniques.

Moral & Spiritual Growth

Instilling timeless ethics, able spiritual guidance, and a service-minded approach to life.

Our Learning Environment

The campus of Sree Sankara Vidyamandiram is designed to be a safe, supportive, and highly inspiring setting for youth. We prioritize a student-friendly environment where every space—from our well-ventilated classrooms to specialized activity support areas—is optimized for learning and growth.

With an accessible Principal’s room and collaborative Staff rooms, our administration and educators remain closely connected to the daily academic life, ensuring uncompromised discipline and continuous mentorship.

Sponsor a Child's Education

Sree Sankara Vidyamandiram is deeply rooted in Indian traditions and values. Many of our bright students come from economically backward families in our region.
